We buy surplus

Electronic components and aircraft parts. Excess stock, E&O, surplus lots, and bankruptcy or liquidation lots. Send a stock list and get a written offer.

What we buy

  • Excess and E&O inventory from OEMs, EMS / contract manufacturers and repair shops: ICs, memory, FPGAs/CPLDs, passives, relays, connectors, power parts, sensors.
  • Industrial automation: PLC CPUs and I/O, drives, servo amplifiers, HMIs (Siemens, Omron, Mitsubishi, Allen-Bradley, Yaskawa and others).
  • Aircraft parts: surplus consumables, expendables and rotables with traceable paperwork (8130-3 / EASA Form 1 or trace to the last operator).
  • Whole lots from bankruptcy estates, receivers, liquidators, plant closings and auctions — we can bid on the full lot.
  • Consignment: keep ownership, we list and sell your stock to our buyers; terms by agreement.
  • Finder's fee: know of a lot? We pay 3% of our purchase price on lots we buy, after resale.

How it works

  1. Send your stock list (Excel or CSV is best): part number, manufacturer, quantity, date code, condition, packaging, location.
  2. We review the list against live market demand and send a written offer — for the lines we want or for the whole lot.
  3. If you accept, we pay by wire before pickup or on agreed milestones. Photos or inspection of the stock may be requested first.
  4. We arrange pickup and freight. You get a bill of sale.

What helps us offer more: factory-sealed reels, trays and tubes; original labels; recent date codes; Certificates of Conformance; storage in a controlled environment; for aircraft parts, complete trace and tags.

What we do not buy: ITAR / military-controlled items, parts without legal title, and anything from sanctioned parties.
