Common Questions

Do you stock parts?

No. We source each order when you send it, so you are quoted on parts that are actually available.

How fast is a quote?

We aim to provide quotes the same business day whenever possible.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, there is a minimum order of $250 before freight.

What are your payment methods?

We require payment by credit card or bank wire before shipment.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We source worldwide and ship to your facility under our name.

Who handles export paperwork?

We check every order against U.S. export rules before it ships, and we tell you what documents your country needs at import.

Can you provide 8130-3 or EASA Form 1?

Often, yes. Tick the paperwork box on the form and we quote only parts that come with the certificate you need.

What if a part is not available?

We tell you promptly, and where there is an approved alternative we quote that instead.

How do I follow up on an order?

Email or message us with your reference number. We stay your contact until the parts arrive.
