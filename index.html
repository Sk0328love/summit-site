    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    How quickly will I receive a quote?

    Our target is the same business day whenever possible. Urgent and AOG requests receive priority. Difficult or obsolete parts can take longer, and we’ll keep you informed while we search.

    Can I send a large parts list?

    Yes. Send one part number or an entire BOM or parts list. You’ll have one point of contact for the request.

    Where do the parts come from?

    We source through independent supply partners in the United States and internationally. Your quote identifies the offered part, condition, availability and applicable documentation.

    Are the parts genuine?

    We quote parts that our supply partners represent as genuine. If traceability, certification, manufacturer documentation or specific paperwork is required, tell us before ordering and we will confirm what is available in writing.

    How do I pay?

    After you accept a quote, we send payment instructions. Payment can be made by credit card through a secure payment link or by bank wire. Payment is required before shipment.

    How does shipping work?

    Orders generally ship directly from the supplying warehouse to your location. Freight is shown separately on the quote and tracking is provided when available.

    For international orders, the buyer is responsible for applicable customs charges, duties and taxes unless the quote specifically states otherwise.

    What if there is a problem with my order?

    Contact us promptly after delivery. Returns, replacements and refunds are subject to the terms stated on your quote and the applicable supplier terms. Installed, altered or used parts generally cannot be returned.

    Do you offer credit terms?

    Not currently. We keep the process simple: quote, acceptance, payment and shipment. There is no account setup required.

    What parts don’t you source?

    We do not sell military or ITAR controlled parts. Transactions are subject to applicable U.S. export laws and screening requirements. We may request end use, end user and destination information before accepting an order.
